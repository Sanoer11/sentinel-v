All voices are synthetic (Gnani Timbre); radio static and band-limiting are simulated. Transcripts are the ones saved from the measured runs — nothing on this page is re-heard or edited. Known limits, measured: a clipped “Runway” or “Crossing” at the start of a readback is often lost from the audio itself; a misheard callsign digit looks exactly like a real pilot error (the alert asks to confirm); hold times are assumptions; ground-position incursions need a surveillance feed, not radio. Decision support only — the controller decides.
